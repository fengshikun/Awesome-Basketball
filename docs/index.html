A NOTE ON THE COLLECTION

A clearer view of
basketball intelligence.

Help grow the library ↗

This library connects work on actions, events, players, tactics, skills, and coaching. Basketball-focused studies sit alongside multi-sport benchmarks that include basketball, with their scope clearly labeled.

Entries are grouped by venue publication year. Earlier preprints are recorded separately, and unpublished work is marked. Project resources are distinguished from peer-reviewed papers. Each entry links to primary sources.

Related datasets can share footage and annotations. Counts from multi-sport or multimodal studies should not be read as basketball-video-only counts. Access and licensing remain with the original authors.

Curated by fengshikun. Inspired by Awesome-AQA and its research browser.
